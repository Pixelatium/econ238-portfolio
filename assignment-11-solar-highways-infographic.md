# Solar Highways

## What if the road above our cars generated electricity?

![Solar Highways infographic. A concept rendering shows a solar canopy over a multi-lane highway, labelled not an existing installation. Panels report a one-kilometre base case with a 12-metre canopy: 1.68 MW capacity, 2.35 GWh a year, $7.42M upfront, roughly 60-year simple payback; the build-up from 12,000 square metres of canopy to 2.352 GWh of output; a cost breakdown where structure is $5.40M or 73% against $2.02M of PV, with a 25-year net present value of negative $5.67M; and arguments for land reuse, public benefits and smart pilots, closing on whether reduced structural costs and verified public benefits could make a solar highway worth building.]({{ '/assets/images/solar-highways-infographic.png' | relative_url }})
